An AI-built trend forecast

Using AI to build an interactive S/S 27 trend forecast in a few days.

Role

Trend research and creative direction

Tools

Claude AI · HTML/CSS

Timeline

Concept to launch in days

Deliverable

Multi-page interactive forecast

Seasonal trend presentations are how creative directors communicate vision to design teams, merchandisers, and retail partners. Traditionally, they take weeks.

They usually mean rounds of layout revisions and a static PDF or slide deck that feels flat next to the trends themselves. I wanted to see whether I could build something richer, an interactive web-based trend experience, in a fraction of the time by using AI for the build.

AI as creative partner

I used Claude to write the code. The research, the themes, the color and material stories and every design decision were mine. The AI handled the technical build and let me try ideas quickly, a bit like directing a production team that turns feedback around in minutes.

I made the creative calls. The AI made it possible to try ten versions in an afternoon.

On directing the build

The process

1 · Strategic foundation

It started with trend research: market analysis, comp shopping and cultural signals. Three themes came out of it, Retro Revival, Earth Elements and Artisan Craft, each with its own palette, material story and mood.

2 · Directing the AI build

With the direction set, I art-directed the build: typography, color, image layouts, animation timing and the pacing of each page.

3 · Iterative refinement

A layout change that would normally take a day of back-and-forth took minutes. Weeks of production became days, without cutting corners on the craft.

Why it matters

When execution gets cheaper, taste and clear direction matter more. A director who can explain exactly what they want can now get to a finished, interactive piece much faster.
