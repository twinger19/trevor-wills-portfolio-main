How I run a design team

01

Start with the brief

Before concepts go on the wall, I want to know the customer, the price point, the retailer and what the brand won’t do. Most wasted samples start with a fuzzy brief.

02

Bring merchandising in early

My best seasons had design and merchandising working through trade-offs in the first month, before the calendar made the decision for us.

03

Write it down

Design guides, standards and training toolkits for new designers, so the team can run a season well without waiting on me for every call.
