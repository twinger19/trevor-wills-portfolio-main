Running a licensed brand portfolio

Running the seasonal design process for 7+ licensed brands a season, from trend research and concepts through tech packs, sample review and lookbook photography.

Role

Design Director, design lead

Brands

Levi's, Calvin Klein, Tommy Hilfiger +4

Team

Senior and associate designers, US and overseas offices

Portfolio

11 licensed brands + private label

Eleven licensed brands, one design team, one calendar and a shared set of factories. Keeping each brand distinct inside that system was the job.

Each season I directed design for 7+ of the brands in our 11-brand portfolio, from Levi’s workwear Americana to Calvin Klein’s minimalism to Kenneth Cole’s sharper city look. Every collection also had to be approved by the licensor’s own brand team, people who know those brands better than anyone.

I owned that cycle from color and material research through trend boards, concepts, tech packs, sample review and final photography, and I led the designers who carried it out.

The seasonal design process

Each season started 12 to 18 months out with trend research. I worked with forecasting partners and our in-house team to find the color, material and silhouette shifts that made sense across several brands and price points, then turned them into brand-specific trend boards built on market data, consumer behavior and each brand’s history.

Design leadership

Once direction was set, I ran design development: reviewing concepts, giving feedback to designers in the US and overseas as the work came in, and keeping each brand consistent across product categories. The goal was a collection that moved the brand forward and could still be made at the right cost.

An idea that can’t be produced on the calendar and at the cost isn’t finished yet.

Technical specification & production

I worked with technical teams to turn designs into tech packs with precise specs, responsibly sourced materials and realistic factory timelines. Design, development and sourcing have to work closely here, or the design gets changed on the factory floor.

Lookbooks & photography

Each season ended with lookbook and product photography for showrooms, wholesale and digital. I directed the shoots from concept through final images so the collection told one story everywhere it showed up.
