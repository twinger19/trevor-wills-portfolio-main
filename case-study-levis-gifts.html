Gift Concepts for Levi’s

A holiday gift program for Levi’s: six small collections named for San Francisco streets, built in cut-edge leather, waxed canvas and bandana prints, with kraft packaging for most pieces.

Earlier workFrom my years as a hands-on designer at Randa, before I moved into leading the team.

01 / The idea

Two families, one gift story

The program split into two material families. Sandokan used cut-edge leather with heavy pull-up. Pitstop mixed waxed canvas, wool and heavy milled leather.

Each family broke into small collections named for San Francisco streets, a nod to Levi’s hometown: Lombard and Montgomery for Sandokan, and Haight, Castro, Belden and Battery for Pitstop.

02 / The collections

From wallets to weekenders

The range ran from slimfolds, card cases and smartphone pockets to flasks, coaster sets, travel kits, a bike tool roll, bags, ties, bow ties and pocket squares. Use the arrows to page through the book.

1/14Lombard collection: cut-edge veg leather slimfold, trucker wallet with lanyard, smartphone case and card case.

2/14Lombard collection: leather strap with two buckles, and embossed leather coaster sets in box and tin options.

3/14Montgomery collection: cut-edge color leather with chambray lining, from key lanyard to card case.

4/14Haight collection: pop color stitching on slimfold and zip-around wallets.

5/14Castro collection: leather-wrapped flask, tablet sleeve and mixed material travel kits.

6/14Belden collection: bandana prints with cut-edge leather, plus skinny suspenders.

7/14Battery collection: travel kit, bike lock holster and bike tool roll.

8/14Battery collection: trucker wallet with chain, specialty slimfold and tablet sleeve.

9/14Battery collection: messenger bag, duffel and backpack in waxy canvas.

10/14Battery collection: tote and weekender bags.

11/14Belden neckwear: chambray, plaid and print ties.

12/14Belden neckwear: bandana print ties and pocket square set.

13/14Belden neckwear: bow ties with matching pocket square packaging.

14/14Belden neckwear: self-tie bow ties and pocket squares.

03 / Packaging

Packaging made for gifting

Most pieces came with their own packaging: kraft cards, boxes, wraps and hang tags in earthtone colors with embossing and foil hits, and the red Batwing as the one constant.
