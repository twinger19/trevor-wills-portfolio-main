Damen + Hastings

How a small RAA team built the company’s first owned brand, from a Chicago pop-up to Kohl’s.

Role

Co-founder, brand lead

Mandate

Stand up an owned brand from zero

Team

Cross-functional founding team

Outcome

Pop-up → DTC → Kohl's

RAA leadership asked a small founding team to build a company-owned brand from the ground up. I was on that team, named the brand, and led its brand development.

Act 1 · Brand foundation

The brand is named after the Chicago corner where RAA’s century-old building, a former leather goods factory, still stands. Company-owned labels usually feel corporate, and this one needed to feel independent and part of the neighborhood. Working with design, product, merchandising and leadership, I helped build the visual identity, brand guidelines and creative strategy around craft, material quality and a real relationship with the customer.

Act 2 · Product & brand standards

With design teams across categories, I helped set brand standards for belts, wallets and bags that held the identity together and still left designers room to work. The focus was quality materials, refined details and accessible prices, ready for both pop-ups and wholesale. Instead of a rigid brand book we wrote a short set of guardrails, so the question in a review was “does this strengthen the Damen + Hastings story?” and not “does this match page 47?”

Act 3 · Retail & ecommerce growth

We launched with Chicago pop-ups, refined the model, and grew into direct-to-consumer and then a national wholesale partnership with Kohl’s, including a Made-in-USA collaboration with Pintrill. Going from one pop-up to national retail proved the brand worked commercially across channels.
