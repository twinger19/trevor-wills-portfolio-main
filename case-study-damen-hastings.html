01The brand02Growth
01 / The brand

A company brand that didn’t feel corporate

RAA leadership asked a small founding team to build a company-owned brand from the ground up. I was on that team, named the brand, and led its brand development.

The brand is named after the Chicago corner where RAA’s century-old building, a former leather goods factory, still stands. It needed to feel independent and part of the neighborhood. Working with design, product, merchandising and leadership, I helped build the visual identity, brand guidelines and product standards around craft, material quality and accessible prices.

02 / Growth

From one pop-up to national retail

We launched with Chicago pop-ups, refined the model, and grew into direct-to-consumer and then a national wholesale partnership with Kohl’s, including a Made-in-USA collaboration with Pintrill.
