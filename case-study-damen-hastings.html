Launching Damen + Hastings

How a small RAA team built the company’s first owned brand, from a Chicago pop-up to Kohl’s.

01 / The brand

A company brand that didn’t feel corporate

RAA leadership asked a small founding team to build a company-owned brand from the ground up. I was on that team, named the brand, and led its brand development.

The brand is named after the Chicago corner where RAA’s century-old building, a former leather goods factory, still stands. It needed to feel independent and part of the neighborhood. Working with design, product, merchandising and leadership, I helped build the visual identity, brand guidelines and product standards around craft, material quality and accessible prices.

02 / The brand book

Writing the brand down first

Before the first product, I worked with a small team on the brand book. It sets out the story, mission and values, maps Damen + Hastings against the brands around it, gives the voice a set of always, yet and never rules, and builds five lifestyle moods, a pricing philosophy and a picture of the customer. It gave the product and packaging work that followed a shared reference point.

03 / The product

A lean line, done well

The book’s line list philosophy kept the range lean: a few core constructions in good raw materials, with newness coming through color and material updates instead of new silhouettes. Concepts like a magnetic tech folio and a clean backpack explored where the brand could stretch, while the core line focused on wallets and card cases.

04 / Packaging & display

Telling the story at the shelf

A new brand has to explain itself in a few seconds at retail. Our packaging designer rendered the in-store shop, fixtures and packaging from my input and direction: a branded shop with the mission front and center, hang cards for belts and ties, and window boxes that show the wallet inside.

05 / Growth

From one pop-up to national retail

We launched with Chicago pop-ups, refined the model, and grew into direct-to-consumer and then a national wholesale partnership with Kohl’s, including a Made-in-USA collaboration with Pintrill. Along the way, I helped supply the product content for the brand’s online pages.

The line list we presented to Kohl’s covered wallets, card cases, pouches, a sling bag, and brushed twill caps and ivies.
