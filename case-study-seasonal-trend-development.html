Seasonal trend development

Building the trend direction for 7+ heritage brands, twice a year.

Role

Design Director, Creative Lead

Scope

7+ heritage brands, bi-annual

Team

Design, merchandising & sales

Deliverables

Research, trend books, showrooms

Each season the team needed one trend story that could work across 7+ heritage brands, each with its own identity, customer and retail partners.

Spotting trends was the easy part. The hard part was one seasonal point of view that felt true to every brand. I ran the process end to end, from market research and comp shopping through trend books, team alignment and the showroom presentations to retail partners.

Research & discovery

Every season started with research: comp shopping trips, trend services and ideas from outside the category. I was looking for the shifts in culture, materials and color that would matter 12 to 18 months out, then turned them into a seasonal direction every brand could design from.

Competitive analysis

I organized and led comp shopping sessions that documented price architecture, material quality, display and assortment gaps. What we found shaped the design direction, and also how we merchandised and presented each collection to retail partners.

A good trend book gives the team enough clarity and confidence to start designing the next morning.

On trend leadership

Trend books & seasonal narratives

The trend book was the center of each season. It turned months of research into color palettes, material direction, silhouettes, hardware trends and mood boards, tailored to each brand and its retail partners. I designed and directed the books so the whole team could design straight from them.

From book to showroom

Each season ended in front of the client. I turned the trend book into a showroom presentation that got design, merchandising and sales telling the same story, then walked retail partners through it.
